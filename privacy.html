Privacy - plain language

Your information is not the product.

This notice describes this fictional sample only, not a live hospital's patient-data policy.

No patient information collected

There is no appointment form, login, payment, medical record upload or chatbot. We do not ask for your name, phone number, symptoms or reports.

Language preference on your device

Your English/Hinglish choice is saved in local browser storage on this device, if available. It is not sent to a database. Clearing site data removes it.

Hosting and external resources

The hosting provider may process ordinary technical logs such as IP address and request details under its own policy. Fonts load from Google Fonts. No analytics, advertising trackers or map embeds are added by this demo.

Nothing to submit or book

Call, WhatsApp and map actions are disabled. No appointment is confirmed here. Use only the verified channel of a real hospital when seeking care.

Before becoming a real hospital site

A hospital must approve the content, contact details and a policy that matches its actual data practices. Any future form or third-party booking tool needs a separate privacy and security review.
